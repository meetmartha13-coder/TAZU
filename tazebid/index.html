🏆 How TAZE BID works

A simple, transparent auction experience.

1. Discover
Browse real clothes and shoes listed by sellers.

2. Place a bid
Sign in and bid at least the displayed minimum next bid.

3. Win the auction
When the timer ends, the highest valid bidder is the provisional winner. Seller confirmation and payment collection are a separate next step.

Never send money directly to a seller until TAZE BID's secure checkout and winner-notification workflow is enabled.
